About Lentera Global

We are a creative collective dedicated to exploring the world through meaningful narratives. From remote mountains to urban centers, we capture authentic stories that illuminate culture, humanity, and the shared threads that connect us all.

Inspired by the spirit of discovery, our work spans travel films, short documentaries, and cultural features designed to spark curiosity and empathy.
